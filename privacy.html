Privacy

Last updated October 12, 2026.

What we collect

When you order, you give your name, phone number, optional email, delivery address (for deliveries) and any details you type for Mili. These are stored in the shop database so Mili can make and deliver your order.

Payments

Payments are handled by Stripe on its own secure checkout page. Your card number never reaches this website or Mili. See Stripe’s privacy policy.

How it is used

Only to make your order and contact you about it. Your information is never sold or shared for marketing.

Your browser

Your bag and your motion setting are saved in your own browser (local storage). The site uses no tracking cookies or ads.

Questions or deletion

Message Mili on Instagram to ask about or delete your information.
